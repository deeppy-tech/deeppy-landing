The value chain

From agro waste to construction material

AGRO.Build.ER traces every transformation step, capturing data from secondary raw material sourcing through processing to the final construction product.

Secondary Raw Material
Agricultural waste sourced and characterized. Quality grading, density, fragmentation and dust residue documented.
Processing
Waste transformation into bio-based building components. Manufacturing parameters, energy use and curing conditions tracked.
Construction Product
Construction material with full traceability. Technical performance and environmental data consolidated in a Digital Product Passport.
The platform

The Digital Product Passport platform for the agro-based construction supply chain

DeePPy provides the infrastructure to collect, structure, and share product data along the entire value chain, enabling regulatory compliance and supply chain transparency.

AI-powered data extraction

Upload Document of Transportation, testing reports and technical sheets. The platform extracts and structures product data automatically, reducing manual entry by over 80%.

3-level DPP architecture

Model, Batch, and Item level passports with data inheritance. Each level adds granularity while maintaining full traceability from product type to individual piece.

Supply chain mapping

Interactive visualization of the entire supply chain. Every component traced back to its origin with supplier data, material sourcing and geographical provenance documented.

ESPR compliance by design

Aligned with EU 2024/1781 (ESPR) requirements for construction products. The DPP structure follows the regulatory framework, preparing manufacturers for upcoming obligations.

Project

AGRO.Build.ER

AGRO.Build.ER develops digital product passports for bio-based construction materials, enabling full supply chain traceability from agricultural waste and secondary raw materials to construction products. The platform addresses ESPR EU 2024/1781 compliance for the construction sector, documenting material provenance, manufacturing parameters, and environmental performance data throughout the value chain. Keywords: digital product passport construction, bio-based building materials traceability, secondary raw material from agricultural waste, DPP construction products, supply chain transparency building sector, ESPR compliance construction materials, circular economy construction, agro waste valorization building products.
